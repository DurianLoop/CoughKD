\section{Conclusion}

We introduced \textsc{CoughKD}, a research protocol and manuscript draft for distilling audio foundation-model teachers into compact cough screening students. The planned study emphasizes cross-dataset validation, calibration, and deployment metrics, which are essential for turning cough audio modeling from a high-accuracy demo into a credible edge-screening system. The next step is implementation: fixed preprocessing, subject-disjoint splits, teacher fine-tuning, student distillation, and replacement of all target tables with measured results.
