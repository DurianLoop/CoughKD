\title{\textbf{CoughKD: Distilling Audio Foundation Models into Compact Cough Screening Networks}}
\author{Anonymous Authors\\Anonymous Institution}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Audio-based respiratory screening has become a promising low-cost pathway for monitoring cough-related diseases, yet the strongest audio foundation models remain too expensive for continuous mobile or edge deployment. We present \textsc{CoughKD}, a teacher--student framework that transfers disease-discriminative and robustness-preserving knowledge from pretrained audio foundation models to compact cough screening networks. A BEATs/AST/PANNs teacher is fine-tuned on cough and respiratory audio with subject-disjoint training splits, while MobileNetV3, EfficientNet, BC-ResNet, and ECAPA-style students are optimized using response-, feature-, attention-, and relation-level distillation. To reduce the common overclaiming risk in cough-based diagnosis, we evaluate models under in-domain, cross-dataset, calibration, subgroup, and efficiency protocols. The proposed study targets a deployable model that preserves at least 95\% of teacher AUROC while reducing parameters by more than 10$\times$ and CPU latency by more than 5$\times$. This manuscript is a research protocol and paper draft: all numerical results marked as targets must be replaced with controlled experimental measurements before submission.
\end{abstract}
