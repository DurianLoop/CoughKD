# 5.85 Selective Coverage Frontier

## Purpose

Test another no-download route:

> Can confidence/entropy/margin-based selective prediction produce a stable 3-5 point gain on large external targets?

This is not a full-coverage classifier claim. It asks whether an abstention/triage framing might be a viable ICASSP contribution.

## Literature Boundary

Selective prediction and abstention under shift are mature topics. Confidence, entropy, and margin are common selection signals, and recent work has studied selective classification under distribution shift and AUC-oriented selective classification.

Useful anchors:

- `https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06251`
- `https://openreview.net/forum?id=JXzgr1X9o3`
- `https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00113`

Therefore, selective coverage can only support the paper if it provides strong cough-specific evidence, not as a standalone novelty claim.

## Selection Rules

The audit uses no target labels for selection.

Selectors:

- max probability,
- low entropy,
- top-1/top-2 margin.

Coverage:

- `0.5`, `0.7`, `0.8`, `0.9`.

Metrics:

- selective AUROC,
- delta vs the method full target AUROC,
- delta vs source-only full target AUROC,
- delta vs source-only on the same retained subset.

## Large-Target Results

Best rows vs source-only full target:

| Target | Method | Selector | Coverage | Selective AUROC | Delta vs source full |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TosCOVID | CE | low entropy | `0.5` | `0.522312` | `+0.020834` |
| TosCOVID | CE | max prob | `0.5` | `0.520063` | `+0.018585` |
| COUGHVID | Candidate F | low entropy | `0.5` | `0.531583` | `+0.016901` |
| COUGHVID | Candidate F | low entropy | `0.7` | `0.530213` | `+0.015531` |

None clear the requested 3-point gate vs source-only full target.

Best rows vs source-only on the same retained subset:

| Target | Method | Selector | Coverage | Delta vs source same subset | Bootstrap CI |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| TosCOVID | Candidate C | margin | `0.5` | `+0.032317` | `[-0.006522, 0.070037]` |
| COUGHVID | Candidate F | low entropy | `0.5` | `+0.025408` | `[0.001429, 0.050665]` |

Interpretation:

- TosCOVID same-subset gain is not useful: absolute selective AUROC is only `0.490906`, below source-only full target `0.501478`, and the CI lower bound is negative.
- COUGHVID same-subset gain is statistically positive in this bootstrap, but it is only `+2.54` points and does not reach the requested 3-point threshold.

Small targets again show much larger apparent gains:

- Virufy: up to `+0.311746` vs source full, but only 10 retained examples at 50% coverage.
- Virufyseg: KD low-entropy 50% coverage reaches `0.764310`, `+0.177409` vs source full, but the target remains small.

## Decision

Selective coverage is not a claimable main route.

Reasons:

- No large/medium full-target comparison clears 3 points.
- The one TosCOVID same-subset 3-point row is an artifact of source-only being weak on that selected subset, not a deployable improvement.
- COUGHVID has a real but sub-threshold selective gain.
- Selective prediction/abstention itself is not novel enough.

Keep this as:

1. a possible triage analysis,
2. an appendix for coverage-risk behavior,
3. a component to revisit only after a stronger representation gate such as HeAR or UKCOVID is available.
