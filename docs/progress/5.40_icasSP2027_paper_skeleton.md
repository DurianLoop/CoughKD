# 5.40 ICASSP 2027 Paper Skeleton：CoughKD-ShiftAudit

## Working Title

**CoughKD-ShiftAudit: Failure Cartography for Ultra-Light Cough Audio Distillation under Dataset Shift**

备选中文标题：

**CoughKD-ShiftAudit：面向数据集漂移的超轻量咳嗽音频蒸馏失败图谱与部署审计**

## One-Sentence Claim

> Knowledge distillation for ultra-light cough audio students can appear stable in aggregate but remain fragile under target composition shift; therefore, deployment-oriented cough KD should be audited with external, slice-level, bootstrap, calibration, probe, and efficiency evidence rather than a single aggregate AUROC.

中文：

> 超轻量咳嗽音频学生模型的 KD 在总体指标上可能看起来略有收益，但在目标子群体和目标组成变化下仍然脆弱，因此端侧咳嗽 KD 不能只看总体 AUROC，而需要外部泛化、slice、bootstrap、校准、probe 和效率的多证据审计。

## Abstract Draft

Large audio models provide useful representations for cough and respiratory sounds, but deploying them on edge devices requires compact students. Knowledge distillation is a natural compression tool, yet its reliability under cough dataset shift remains underexplored. We introduce CoughKD-ShiftAudit, a deployment-oriented audit protocol for ultra-light cough audio distillation. Using Coswara as the source dataset and COUGHVID as the external target, we evaluate CE-only training, vanilla KD, target-consistency distillation, shortcut-suppressed KD, disagreement-gated KD, and probe-adversarial training across multiple seeds. Beyond aggregate AUROC, we report calibration, task and domain probes, sensitivity at high specificity, model size, latency, metadata-defined target slices, and bootstrap target-subset stability. Our results show that the most stable KD variant improves external macro AUROC by only +0.0007 over source-only continuation, while slice-level post-hoc gains can reach substantially larger values. However, target-unlabeled confidence, entropy, and source-relative shift signals fail to reliably select the best method across slices. These findings suggest that cough audio distillation under dataset shift requires failure-oriented auditing before deployment, and that shortcut or calibration improvements alone should not be interpreted as external robustness.

## Contributions

### Contribution 1：问题重定义

不把论文写成：

> New COVID cough diagnosis model.

而是写成：

> Deployment reliability audit for ultra-light cough audio distillation under dataset shift.

这避开了“咳嗽是否真的能诊断 COVID”的强临床 claim 风险。

### Contribution 2：CoughKD-ShiftAudit 协议

协议包含：

- source training: Coswara cough split；
- external target: COUGHVID；
- multi-seed comparison: seeds 7/11/23；
- methods: CE-only、vanilla KD、target-consistency KD、confidence-gated TCD、shortcut-suppressed KD、disagreement-gated KD、probe-adversarial KD；
- metrics: macro AUROC、COVID AUROC、macro AUPRC、ECE、Brier、NLL、COVID sensitivity at 95% specificity、domain probe AUC、task probe AUC、params、checkpoint size、latency；
- stress tests: metadata-defined slices、LOSO guard、bootstrap target subsets。

### Contribution 3：Failure cartography

现有结果显示：

- `tcd_conf035` 是最稳定的方法，但 external macro AUROC 只比 `source_only` 高 `+0.000701`；
- vanilla KD 在 overall macro AUROC 上低于 source-only；
- 25-slice stress test 中 post-hoc best mean delta 为 `+0.008675`，说明局部有可观收益；
- 但 fixed guard 有 `6/25` 负迁移，LOSO guard 有 `7/25` 负迁移；
- bootstrap subsets 中 `tcd_conf035` positive rate 为 `0.74`，但 mean delta 只有 `+0.000823`。

结论：

> 局部收益存在，但当前无标签目标域信号无法可靠发现它们。

### Contribution 4：Metric disagreement

现有结果显示：

- candidate A/B 的 ECE 最低，但 external macro AUROC 更差；
- candidate C 的 Brier/NLL 最好，但 macro AUROC 仍低于 source-only；
- domain probe AUC 较低不必然意味着 external AUROC 更高；
- efficiency 很强，但不能弥补泛化证据不足。

结论：

> calibration、probe、external ranking、deployment efficiency 必须同时报告；单一指标会误导部署判断。
