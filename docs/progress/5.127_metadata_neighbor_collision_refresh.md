## New adjacent works to track

### Metadata as text/context in respiratory audio

TIAMO-style text-image-audio multimodal respiratory disease classification is
close because patient metadata or clinical context can be converted into text
and fused with respiratory audio representations.

Collision assessment:

- close to broad metadata-aided respiratory modeling;
- not a deployment-time router between pre-existing transfer strategies;
- no evidence in this pass of a field-semantics rule that allows symptom or
  acquisition slices while sending demographic-only slices to stacking;
- no inverted/all-slice/no-slice semantic controls.

Boundary impact:

- do not claim first metadata-to-text or metadata-context respiratory model;
- keep the remaining unit as transfer-strategy routing constrained by metadata
  field type.

### HeAR plus demographic metadata stacking

PulmoVec-style pipelines are close because respiratory foundation embeddings can
be combined with demographic metadata and a stacked classifier. This is close to
the `logistic_stacking` side of the current semantic router.

Collision assessment:

- close to demographic-metadata stacking;
- not a rule deciding when demographic metadata should not become a slice-gated
  transfer variable;
- not a symptom/acquisition-vs-demographic safety prior over transfer strategy.

Boundary impact:

- do not claim first demographic metadata stacking;
- describe stacking as an existing modeling family used as one conservative
  branch of the router.

### Demographic acoustic attributes in cough

`Decoding Gender in Cough Sounds` is close because it shows demographic
attributes may be inferable from cough acoustics. This strengthens the caution
that demographic fields can act as confounds under dataset shift.

Collision assessment:

- close to demographic metadata and fairness/interpretability concerns;
- not a transfer-routing method;
- not a slice-gating vs stacking policy.

Boundary impact:

- demographic-only fields should not be framed as clinically safe transfer
  gates;
- the semantic router's caution around age/sex-like fields remains appropriate.

## Current interpretation

This refresh makes the non-claim boundary stricter but does not find a direct
collision for the current semantic-router claim. The crowded areas are:

- metadata-aided respiratory classification;
- metadata-as-text/context fusion;
- demographic metadata stacking;
- demographic attribute decoding from cough.

The still-plausible contribution remains narrower:

> target metadata fields are not interchangeable transfer variables; symptom or
> acquisition fields may support slice-gated transfer, while demographic-only
> fields route to calibration stacking, with semantic inversions serving as
> negative controls.

This is not final proof of novelty. It is a dated collision audit to support the
current claim boundary until a broader systematic review or new direct collision
appears.
