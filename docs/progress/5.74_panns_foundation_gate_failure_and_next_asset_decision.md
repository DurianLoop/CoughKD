## Related-Work Boundary

Recent foundation-audio and respiratory-audio work makes the broad novelty space crowded:

- BEATs learns general audio representations with acoustic tokenizers and reports strong AudioSet/ESC-50 performance: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09058`
- HeAR trains health acoustic representations on 313M two-second clips and evaluates on 33 health acoustic tasks: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02522`
- OPERA introduces open respiratory acoustic foundation pretraining and benchmarking: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16148`
- Interspeech 2025 respiratory pretraining work reports that AudioSet-pretrained models can be more effective than respiratory-only pretraining on OPERA-style tasks: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15307`

Implication:

- We cannot claim novelty as "foundation embeddings for respiratory/cough audio."
- We also cannot claim "first foundation-to-student cough distillation" because earlier progress notes already identify Coughprint-style work in that area.
- A defensible ICASSP 2027 route needs either a verified positive method under external shift or a strong guarded-transfer/failure-audit contribution.

## Decision

Do not launch a PANNs-only foundation-KD positive-claim experiment.

Reason:

- Frozen PANNs improves all-Tos oracle only to roughly the same level as the previous source-CE student embedding upper bound.
- It does not solve the critical Tos env-1 failure slice.
- Distilling this representation into a smaller student is unlikely to produce a defensible 3-5 point Tos improvement.

## Next Decision Point

The remaining positive-method routes require user approval because they involve downloading or integrating larger assets:

1. **HeAR/OPERA-style health respiratory embeddings**
   - Most scientifically aligned with cough/respiratory transfer.
   - Highest chance of improving env 1 if public assets are usable.
   - Requires checking model availability and likely downloading weights.

2. **BEATs/AST general audio embeddings**
   - Strong general audio baselines.
   - Less health-specific than HeAR/OPERA.
   - Requires local checkpoints and wrappers.

3. **Stop positive-method hunting on Tos and pivot**
   - Treat Tos as a hard negative external target.
   - Build the ICASSP paper around guarded transfer, failure-slice auditing, and when not to distill.
   - Lower headline-gain upside, but more consistent with current evidence.

Current recommendation:

- Ask the user before downloading or integrating new foundation assets.
- If approved, next low-compute gate should be HeAR/OPERA frozen embedding oracle on Tos env 1.
- Do not design another KD method until a foundation embedding gate clears `0.60` env-1 AUROC.
