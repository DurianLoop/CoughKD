## 2. New Literature Check: What Is Already Taken

### 2.1 Cough representation distillation is already taken

The 2025 paper **Coughprint: Distilled Cough Representations From Speech Foundation Model Embeddings** directly uses KD to train edge-compute student models for cough measurement and classification. It reports that a student preserves cough representation capability from a speech foundation teacher and achieves strong downstream results with over 200x fewer parameters.

Implication:

- We should not frame our novelty as simply "distilling speech/audio foundation representations into an edge cough model."
- That direction overlaps too much with Coughprint.

Source:

- https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11024165/
- https://colab.ws/articles/10.1109%2Ftim.2025.3568985

### 2.2 Respiratory foundation/audio-language models are moving fast

Recent respiratory audio foundation and audio-language work includes OPERA, CaReAQA, AcuLa, RespLLM, and TRIAGE-style adaptive inference.

Important examples:

- **OPERA, NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks**: open respiratory acoustic foundation model pretraining and benchmark system using multi-source respiratory audio.
- **CaReAQA, 2025**: cardiac/respiratory audio question answering and audio-language diagnostic reasoning.
- **AcuLa, 2025/2026**: aligns audio encoders with medical language models acting as semantic teachers, reporting strong gains across cardio-respiratory tasks.
- **TRIAGE, 2026**: adaptive test-time scaling for zero-shot respiratory audio classification, routing uncertain samples to richer reasoning stages.

Implication:

- A pure "stronger teacher model" claim is risky.
- A pure "audio-language model for respiratory diagnosis" claim is also risky.
- The field is already moving toward foundation models, audio-language alignment, and adaptive inference.

Sources:

- OPERA: https://opera-benchmark.github.io/
- OPERA paper: https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/2f803abdcad9de35b45d5a656dade45c-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf
- CaReAQA: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01199
- AcuLa: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04847
- TRIAGE: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12647

### 2.3 Domain-shift KD is studied, but not solved for our exact setting

There are general KD-under-shift works:

- **Weight-Averaged KD**, 2023: weight averaging can improve KD under domain shift.
- **KD^3**, 2023: distribution shift matters when distilling with webly collected images.
- **Revisiting KD under Distribution Shift**, 2023/2024: benchmarks many KD methods and finds poor teaching behavior under shifts; complex KD and augmentation often give limited gains.
- **ShiftKD**, 2025: benchmarking KD under distribution shift.

Implication:

- "KD under distribution shift can fail" is not novel by itself.
- "Domain-aware KD" is not novel by itself.
- But a cough/respiratory-audio-specific protocol that predicts KD failure from unlabeled target-domain signals before deployment still appears underexplored from the current search.

Sources:

- WAKD: https://huggingface.co/papers/2309.11446
- KD^3: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11469
- Revisiting KD under shift: https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2312.16242
- ShiftKD: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S089360802500718X

## 3. Candidate ICASSP Direction After This Check

### Working title

**CoughKD-Guard: Target-Unlabeled Transfer Risk Auditing for Ultra-Light Cough Distillation Under Dataset Shift**

Earlier working name was `KD-Triage`, but this should be avoided because a 2026 speaker verification paper already uses **Triage Knowledge Distillation** for a different KD objective. Our direction is not posterior-group triage during training; it is pre-deployment transfer-risk auditing and model selection under target-domain shift.

### Core idea

Instead of proposing another KD loss, we propose a **failure-aware deployment protocol**:

1. Train compact cough students with CE and several bounded KD variants on a source dataset.
2. Before using target labels, compute unlabeled target-domain signals:
   - target prediction confidence
   - normalized entropy
   - predicted class prior shift
   - target COVID probability mass
   - probe-readable dataset/domain information
   - disagreement between CE, KD, and adapted variants
3. Use these signals to predict whether KD is likely to help or hurt external AUROC.
4. If risk is high, route to safer alternatives:
   - CE/source-only student
   - conservative TCD
   - abstain / human-review / higher-compute teacher
5. Evaluate whether this protocol reduces negative transfer under Coswara -> COUGHVID and later Coswara/COUGHVID -> DiCOVA.

This is different from:

- Coughprint: they distill universal cough representations; we analyze and predict KD failure under dataset shift.
- OPERA/AcuLa/TRIAGE: they focus on foundation or zero-shot adaptive inference; we focus on compact distilled student deployment and KD risk triage.
- Triage KD for speaker verification: it partitions teacher posterior probabilities during KD training; we do not propose a new posterior partition loss, but a target-unlabeled transfer guard for already trained compact cough students.
- Generic KD-under-shift papers: they benchmark or improve KD broadly; we focus on respiratory/cough audio, target-unlabeled pre-deployment risk signals, and lightweight deployment.

## 5. Proposed Next Innovation Gate

To make KD-Triage claimable, the next experiment must satisfy all of these:

1. **More target settings**
   - Add DiCOVA if possible.
   - Create at least two target protocols:
     - Coswara -> COUGHVID
     - Coswara -> DiCOVA
     - optional: COUGHVID -> Coswara if labels are harmonized.

2. **Pre-deployment prediction**
   - The triage score must be computed without target labels.
   - It may use target audio and model predictions, but not target ground-truth.

3. **Predictive validity**
   - Across seeds/methods/datasets, triage score should correlate with actual external AUROC delta.
   - Required preliminary gate:
     - Spearman or Pearson correlation >= 0.5 with external macro AUROC delta across at least 15 method-seed-target points.

4. **Decision utility**
   - A simple triage policy must outperform always-use-KD and always-use-CE/source-only selection.
   - Gate:
     - mean external macro AUROC selected by triage >= best fixed baseline - 0.001
     - and negative-transfer rate lower than vanilla KD by at least 30%.

5. **No open-ended loss search**
   - We do not add unlimited KD variants.
   - We use existing CE/KD/TCD/A/B/C results plus at most one additional target dataset.

## 7. Additional Novelty Stress Test

The current claim should be stress-tested against:

- **Triage Knowledge Distillation for Speaker Verification, 2026**: name conflict and a KD objective based on target/confusion/background posterior grouping. Avoid the `KD-Triage` name and do not claim posterior triage as novelty.
- **TRIAGE, 2026**: adaptive test-time scaling for zero-shot respiratory audio. Different because it routes per-sample compute across zero-shot reasoning tiers, whereas CoughKD-Guard selects among compact pre-trained student models before deployment using target-unlabeled transfer-risk signals.
- **Meta-DMoE, 2022**: target-domain unlabeled data is used for meta-distillation from mixtures of experts. Different because we are not adapting a student with MoE teachers; we audit negative transfer risk among already trained ultra-light cough students.
- **WAKD / KD under shift / ShiftKD**: these study KD under distribution shift broadly. Different only if we can demonstrate a cough-audio-specific, target-unlabeled deployment guard with decision utility across at least two target datasets.

Therefore, the novelty is still conditional. It becomes plausible only if the next external target confirms that CoughKD-Guard predicts or avoids negative transfer better than always-use-KD / always-use-CE / always-use-source-only.
