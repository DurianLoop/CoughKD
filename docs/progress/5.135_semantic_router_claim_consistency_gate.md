# 5.135 Semantic-Router Claim Consistency Gate

Date: 2026-06-09

## Purpose

The semantic-router evidence now has enough moving parts that the paper can
accidentally drift into an overclaim. I added an automated claim-consistency
audit to keep the main paper, supplement, readiness gate, and claim dossier
aligned.

## New artifact

- Script: `scripts/audit_semantic_router_claim_consistency.py`
- Report: `runs/semantic_router_claim_consistency/SEMANTIC_ROUTER_CLAIM_CONSISTENCY.md`
- JSON: `runs/semantic_router_claim_consistency/semantic_router_claim_consistency.json`

The audit checks:

- main-paper anchors for row-level `+3.75` / `+3.99` and conservative
  subject-grouped TosCOVID `+2.94`;
- main-paper non-clinical boundary text;
- supplement subject-grouped and non-claim anchors;
- claim dossier status;
- readiness status;
- forbidden overclaim phrases such as first-metadata, clinical utility,
  label-free, general KD superiority, and guarantee language unless explicitly
  negated.

## Integration

The submission-readiness gate now requires the claim-consistency audit to pass.
The claim dossier also includes a `Claim consistency` requirement.
