## Novelty Boundary

Do not claim:

- first health/respiratory acoustic foundation model,
- first foundation embedding for cough or respiratory audio,
- first foundation-to-student cough representation distillation,
- first target-unlabeled audio adaptation.

Those spaces are already crowded by HeAR, OPERA, Coughprint-style work, BEATs/AST/PANNs, PADA/TTA/SFDA, and respiratory KD papers already logged in earlier progress notes.

Potential claim if the next gate succeeds:

> A guarded distillation protocol that first proves whether a health/respiratory foundation embedding contains target-slice signal, then only distills when the slice-level gate predicts transferability.

This is narrower than foundation-model novelty and better aligned with our current evidence.
