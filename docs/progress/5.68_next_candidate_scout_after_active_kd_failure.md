## Candidate E Sketch

Working name:

> Transfer-Gap Audited KD (TGA-KD)

Hypothesis:

The problem is not that cough activity is weak; Candidate D showed that active-quality source weighting is insufficient. A better candidate may need to identify source examples whose teacher responses are likely to transfer to an ultra-light student under public-dataset shift.

Candidate signal family:

- teacher entropy / margin;
- student-teacher disagreement during early student training;
- class-region coverage of teacher predictions;
- source example proximity to external target representation distribution without target labels;
- per-class prediction histogram balance to avoid collapsing KD pressure into majority/healthy regions.

Potential novelty boundary:

Not a generic entropy KD claim. The possible novelty would be:

> A cough-shift-specific transfer-gap audit and weighting rule for ultra-light KD, validated on COUGHVID and Tos with explicit failure gates.

Collision risks:

- Generic transfer-gap KD;
- entropy/adaptive KD;
- dataset-quality-for-KD work;
- target-domain KD and selective adapter KD.

To be claimable, Candidate E must combine:

- cough public-dataset shift;
- ultra-light student;
- source/target unlabeled diagnostics;
- external target validation;
- no target-label tuning.
