## 新增脚本

### `scripts/run_ukcovid_after_audio_windows.cmd`

用途：在 UK 49.95 GiB split audio archive 下载并解压后，一键执行：

1. `scripts/check_ukcovid_audio_ready.py`
2. 正式重建 `manifests/ukcovid_open_test_external.csv`，使用 `--recursive-audio-search`
3. Coswara vs UK overlap audit
4. `scripts/onboard_external_target.py --target-tag ukcovid_open`
5. `scripts/audit_claim_boundary.py`
6. `scripts/audit_submission_readiness.py`

如果音频未 ready，脚本会退出并指向：

```text
runs/ukcovid_open_metadata_audit/UKCOVID_AUDIO_READY_CHECK.md
```

使用方式：

```cmd
cd /d D:\CoughKD\AAAI
scripts\run_ukcovid_after_audio_windows.cmd
```
