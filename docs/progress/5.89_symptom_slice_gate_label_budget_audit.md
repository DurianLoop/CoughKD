# 5.89 Symptom-Slice Gate Label-Budget Audit

## Purpose

Stress-test the best candidate from `5.88`:

> Does the margin-regularized symptom-slice transfer gate remain strong with a realistic low target-label budget?

The previous best setting used a `70%` target calibration split, which is too expensive for a strong "label-efficient" claim.

## Script Updates

Updated:

- `scripts/audit_crossfit_metadata_slice_policy.py`

New optional parameter:

- `--train-per-slice`

Behavior:

- If omitted, the script uses the old random `--train-frac` split.
- If provided, the script samples up to `k` labeled calibration examples per metadata slice and evaluates on all remaining examples.

Updated:

- `scripts/summarize_crossfit_metadata_slice_grid.py`

Change:

- summary tables now show `train_per_slice` and mean actual training-label count.

## Fixed Per-Slice Label Budget

Target:

- COUGHVID

Slice:

- `symptom_resp`

Gate:

- base method: `source_only`
- switch margin: `0.02`
- repeats: `500`

Results:

| Train per slice | Mean actual labels | Mean delta | Median delta | CI low | CI high | P(delta < 3pt) |

| `500` | `1000` | `+0.010772` | `+0.014446` | `-0.020765` | `+0.028849` | `0.982` |
| `100` | `200` | `+0.007716` | `+0.007916` | `-0.043308` | `+0.055785` | `0.754` |
| `50` | `100` | `+0.006380` | `+0.007565` | `-0.046271` | `+0.059611` | `0.756` |

Conclusion:

- Equal per-slice few-shot calibration does **not** preserve the 3-point effect.
- The best fixed budget is only about `+1.56` points mean.

## Proportional Train-Fraction Budget

Same gate, but using random train fractions that preserve target slice proportions in expectation:

| Train fraction | Mean actual labels | Mean delta | Median delta | CI low | CI high | P(delta < 3pt) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `0.7` | `2558` | `+0.045267` | `+0.046392` | `0.000000` | `+0.067211` | `0.078` |
| `0.5` | `1828` | `+0.037214` | `+0.043400` | `-0.028948` | `+0.057994` | `0.200` |
| `0.3` | `1096` | `+0.025742` | `+0.036885` | `-0.033526` | `+0.052254` | `0.436` |

Conclusion:

- `50%` proportional calibration still has mean and median above 3 points, but CI lower bound is negative.
- `30%` proportional calibration has median above 3 points but mean below 3 points.
- `70%` remains the strongest and most stable setting.

## Interpretation

The candidate is still alive, but the label-efficiency claim weakened.

What improved:

- The margin gate remains strong when calibration labels follow the target distribution and the budget is large enough.
- `50%` target calibration gives a nontrivial held-out mean of `+3.72` points.

What failed:

- Fixed per-slice few-shot calibration did not work.
- The method currently needs hundreds to thousands of target labels, not a few-shot budget.
- The best stable result remains COUGHVID-only.

## Revised Candidate Position

Better name:

> Margin-regularized symptom-slice transfer gate.

Avoid overclaiming:

- Do not call it few-shot.
- Do not call it broadly label-efficient yet.

Safer claim shape:

> With a moderate target calibration set, a symptom-conditioned margin gate can reduce negative transfer by selecting between source-only and specialized students across external cough-dataset slices.

Still missing:

- replication on UKCOVID or another large external target,
- lower-budget calibration,
- proof that the novelty is not just symptom-assisted fusion.

## Decision

Do not claim yet.

This audit narrows the candidate:

- promising as a calibration-heavy negative-transfer gate,
- not yet a low-label/few-shot gate,
- still needs external-state validation.
