# 5.88 Margin-Regularized Symptom-Slice Gate

## Purpose

Improve the strongest local candidate from `5.87`.

Problem:

- Metadata-slice transfer gating had promising COUGHVID gains.
- But it was unstable: many repeats still fell below the 3-point threshold.

Hypothesis:

> A slice should switch away from source-only only when the calibration split shows a sufficiently large slice-local gain.

This adds a simple anti-overfitting margin to the label-efficient metadata-slice gate.

## Script Update

Updated:

- `scripts/audit_crossfit_metadata_slice_policy.py`

New parameter:

- `--switch-margin`

Behavior:

- For each metadata slice, compute best method AUROC and base-method AUROC on the calibration half.
- Switch the slice only if `best_auc - base_auc >= switch_margin`.
- Otherwise keep the base method, default `source_only`.

This keeps previous behavior at `--switch-margin 0`.

Also updated:

- `scripts/summarize_crossfit_metadata_slice_grid.py`

Change:

- summary tables now include `switch_margin`.

## Experiment

Target:

- COUGHVID

Slice:

- `symptom_resp`, derived from COUGHVID respiratory-condition metadata.

Policy:

- base method: `source_only`
- train fraction: `0.7`
- min train slice: `100`
- repeated random split crossfit

Margin grid:

- `0`
- `0.005`
- `0.01`
- `0.02`
- `0.03`

## Interpretation

This is a real improvement over the unregularized slice gate:

- mean gain rises from about `+3.10` to `+4.46` points,
- median gain rises from about `+3.25` to `+4.64` points,
- the fraction below 3 points drops from `47.6%` to `10.5%`,
- the fraction at or below zero drops to `4.1%`.

This is now the first local candidate with a repeated held-out mean in the requested 3-5 point range.

## Remaining Problems

Do not claim yet.

Reasons:

- Evidence is currently COUGHVID-only.
- TosCOVID did not reproduce the effect in the previous grid.
- The 1000-repeat CI lower bound is `0.0`, not above 3 points.
- The policy uses `70%` target calibration labels, which is expensive.
- Symptom-assisted cough/COVID models already exist; novelty must be framed as margin-regularized negative-transfer gating, not symptom fusion.

## Candidate Claim Shape

Current best candidate:

> Margin-regularized symptom-slice transfer gate.

More precise possible claim:

> A label-efficient, symptom-conditioned margin gate can decide when to switch from source-only inference to specialized KD/IRM students, reducing negative transfer under external cough-dataset shift.

Current evidence supports:

- promising COUGHVID held-out gains in the 3-5 point range,
- interpretable respiratory-condition slicing,
- margin regularization reduces overfitting.

Current evidence does not yet prove:

- cross-dataset robustness,
- low-label-budget operation,
- novelty beyond existing symptom-assisted cough classification and transfer-source/model selection.

## Next Required Evidence

The next evidence should not be another small local heuristic unless it directly strengthens this candidate.

Best next actions:

1. **UKCOVID audio**: test whether symptom/demographic slice gating stabilizes on a much larger multi-recording external target.
2. **HeAR/OPERA foundation gate**: improve per-slice representation quality and retest the margin gate.
3. **Lower-label-budget variant**: reduce calibration budget from `70%` toward a realistic few-shot-per-slice protocol, but only after confirming the effect can transfer beyond COUGHVID.
