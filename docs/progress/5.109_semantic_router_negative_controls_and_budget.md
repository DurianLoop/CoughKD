# 5.109 Semantic router negative controls and calibration budget

## Purpose

5.108 found a strong candidate:

> Semantic-Constrained Transfer Router: use target metadata semantics to choose whether to deploy metadata slice gating or target-calibrated logistic stacking.

This note hardens the candidate with:

1. semantic-label negative controls;
2. all-slice / no-slice rule ablations;
3. target calibration label-budget sweeps;
4. mean confidence intervals separated from split-distribution lower tails.

No model training, download, or GPU job was run.

## Important statistical distinction

The `ci95_low` column in the router summaries is the 2.5% quantile across random outer splits. It describes tail risk over target splits.

It is not the confidence interval of the mean improvement.

For paper claims, both matter:

- mean CI tests whether the average gain is robustly positive;
- split-tail quantiles test whether the method has occasional negative-transfer failures.

## Claim status after hardening

Provisional internal claim: promising.

Full ICASSP claim: not yet complete.

Why it is now stronger:

- Mean gains are robustly positive on COUGHVID and TosCOVID.
- Negative controls support the semantic mechanism.
- The method improves over the prior target-calibrated controller.
- It is simple and cheap, avoiding new training or foundation assets.

Why it is still not final:

- Only two large external targets are used.
- Split-tail negative-transfer events remain, especially below 30% calibration.
- The literature collision audit is not deep enough to certify novelty.
- A final ICASSP 2027 paper still benefits strongly from UKCOVID/Cambridge/HeAR evidence.

## Current best claim wording

Do not claim:

- "new ensemble method";
- "new domain adaptation method";
- "symptom metadata improves cough COVID classification";
- "label-efficient adaptation."

Candidate claim:

> We show that target metadata is not uniformly safe for transfer routing in COVID cough diagnosis. A semantic constraint over metadata type, distinguishing symptom/acquisition slices from demographic-only slices, reliably selects between slice-gated transfer and calibration stacking, improving average external AUROC by roughly 2-4 points on COUGHVID and TosCOVID while strong negative controls collapse.

This wording is still provisional pending literature review.
