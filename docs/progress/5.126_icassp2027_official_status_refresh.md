# 5.126 ICASSP 2027 official status refresh

## Purpose

Continue the ICASSP 2027 submission-readiness path without starting a large
download or compute step. The previous readiness gate warned that the final
ICASSP 2027 author kit/page rules still needed re-checking. This pass refreshes
that risk against the current official ICASSP 2027 web pages.

## Sources Checked

- Official homepage: https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/
- Official call page: https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/call-for-papers/
- Official CFP PDF linked from the call page:
  https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/12/2026/05/ICASSP-2027-CFP.pdf

## Findings

- The conference is listed for Toronto, Canada, 2027-05-16 to 2027-05-21.
- The official CFP PDF lists:
  - full paper submission deadline: 2026-09-16;
  - acceptance notification: 2027-01-13;
  - final paper deadline: 2027-01-27;
  - author registration deadline: 2027-02-10.
- The official CFP PDF states the current regular-paper page rule:
  - 4 pages for technical content;
  - optional fifth page allowed;
  - the fifth page may contain only references, funding acknowledgements, and
    ethics statements.
- A 2027-specific final author kit/template was not located in this refresh.
