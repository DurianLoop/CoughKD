## Results

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| TosCOVID | 160 | +1.90 | [+1.47, +2.34] | 0.0000 | 0.425 | 0.531 |

Interpretation:

- Semantic routing is paired-bootstrap positive over the nested controller on both targets.
- The win rate is modest because many repeats tie exactly:
  - COUGHVID often already selected `slice_gate`;
  - TosCOVID often already selected `logistic_stacking`.
- The mean gains come from repairing the controller's wrong-family selection events.

This supports the mechanism:

- COUGHVID should consistently allow the symptom slice gate.
- TosCOVID should consistently avoid the demographic age slice and route to stacking.
