# 5.96 Leaf metadata gate negative result

## Purpose

After the slice-balanced control in 5.95, the remaining low-cost question was whether a more specific metadata leaf gate could improve both symptom strata. The tested idea was:

> fit a target-calibrated transfer gate on clinically interpretable metadata leaves such as `symptom_resp x age_bin`, instead of only the coarse `symptom_resp` slice.

This would be a stronger innovation only if it improved the symptom-slice macro result without relying on a broad, overfit metadata tree.

## New artifact

- Script: `scripts/audit_leaf_metadata_gate_policy.py`
- First-pass outputs: `runs/leaf_metadata_gate_policy/`
- Small age-grid outputs: `runs/leaf_metadata_gate_policy_grid/`

The script fits a held-out margin switch policy on leaf groups, then reports:

1. sample-weighted overall macro one-vs-rest AUROC delta;
2. primary symptom-slice macro delta, giving `symptom_resp=false` and `symptom_resp=true` equal weight;
3. per-primary-slice deltas;
4. selected leaf switches.

## First-pass leaf combinations

All runs used:

- target: COUGHVID
- train fraction: 0.7
- switch margin: 0.02
- min train leaf: 100
- repeats: 500
- methods: source_only, ce, kd, candidate_a, candidate_b, candidate_c, candidate_f_artifact_env_irm_ramp

| leaf columns | overall mean delta | p(overall < 3pt) | symptom-slice macro mean delta | p(slice-macro < 3pt) | false-slice mean | true-slice mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| symptom_resp + age_bin | +0.033558 | 0.420 | +0.017707 | 0.748 | +0.021641 | +0.013773 |
| symptom_resp + sex | +0.027580 | 0.544 | +0.006656 | 0.976 | +0.003607 | +0.009705 |
| symptom_resp + cough_detected_bin | +0.036288 | 0.280 | +0.009251 | 0.926 | -0.004059 | +0.022562 |

The age leaf was the best of the three, but it still underperformed the coarse symptom gate on the primary slice-macro criterion.

## Interpretation

This route fails as a claimable innovation.

Compared with the coarse symptom gate:

- coarse symptom gate overall mean: about +0.0446;
- coarse symptom gate symptom-slice macro mean: about +0.0192;
- best age-leaf gate overall mean: about +0.0343;
- best age-leaf gate symptom-slice macro mean: about +0.0176.

The leaf gate makes the `symptom_resp=false` slice less inert, but it sacrifices too much of the `symptom_resp=true` gain. It also introduces many policy variants, making the method harder to defend as a robust, simple negative-transfer gate.

The safer conclusion is:

> Multi-field metadata leaf gating is not the right innovation path for the current evidence package. The stronger pilot result remains the coarse, margin-regularized symptom gate, but that result is still COUGHVID-only and not enough for an ICASSP claim.

## Next search implication

Do not keep expanding COUGHVID metadata rules. The next meaningful progress should come from either:

1. a second external target with structured symptoms and audio/predictions;
2. a new literature-grounded mechanism that does not depend on COUGHVID symptom metadata;
3. UKCOVID or Cambridge acquisition/approval, because local-only evidence has reached a diminishing-returns point.
