# 5.90 Nested Margin Selection Gate

## Purpose

Stress-test the margin-regularized symptom-slice gate from `5.88` and `5.89`.

Concern:

- `switch_margin=0.02` was selected after looking at the COUGHVID margin grid.
- The held-out gain could still include hyperparameter overfitting.

This round uses nested crossfit:

1. outer calibration split,
2. inner train/validation split to choose `switch_margin`,
3. final policy learned on the full outer calibration split,
4. evaluation on untouched outer held-out data.

## Interpretation

Positive:

- Nested selection still gives mean `+3.90` points and median `+4.37` points.
- This argues that the margin-gated symptom-slice effect is not purely a post-hoc fixed-margin artifact.
- The selected margins are diverse; the inner split often chooses no margin, but margin `0.02` remains frequently selected.

Negative:

- Stability is weaker than fixed `switch_margin=0.02`.
- CI lower bound is negative.
- `27.8%` of repeats are still below the 3-point target.
- `7.4%` are at or below zero.
- Evidence remains COUGHVID-only.

## Decision

This strengthens the candidate but still does not complete the claim.

Current candidate status:

- **Alive**: nested held-out mean remains in the 3-5 point range.
- **Not claimable**: stability and cross-target evidence are insufficient.

Revised claim boundary:

> A margin-regularized symptom-slice gate can reduce negative transfer on COUGHVID under moderate target calibration, but it has not yet been shown robust across external cough datasets.

## Next Best Evidence

Further local heuristic tuning is now lower value than external validation.

Required next evidence:

1. UKCOVID audio to test symptom/demographic gating at scale.
2. HeAR/OPERA embeddings to increase slice-level separability and retest the nested gate.
3. A second external target with usable symptoms and audio.
