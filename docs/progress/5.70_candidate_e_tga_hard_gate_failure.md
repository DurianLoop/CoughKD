## Decision

Candidate E fails.

Reason:

- COUGHVID macro AUROC is below the 0.525 continuation gate and below the earlier source-only reference around 0.5146.
- Tos macro AUROC is below the 0.510 continuation gate and below the earlier Tos CE post-hoc reference around 0.5095.
- The source-domain improvement did not transfer.

Do not expand this variant to more seeds. Do not tune its thresholds.

## Implication

The negative result is useful:

- Source-domain teacher reliability and transfer-gap weighting alone are not enough.
- A stronger claim probably needs either explicit shift localization or a mechanism that changes representation learning, not only response-KD sample weights.
- The next candidate should avoid another scalar KD-weight rule unless it has a new source of evidence.

## Recommended Next Fork

Option A: shift-localized representation route.

- Read domain generalization and shortcut-suppression work around audio/cough recording artifacts.
- Look for a method that explicitly separates disease-relevant cough acoustics from device/site/noise proxies.
- This is more likely to support a real ICASSP claim if it improves both COUGHVID and Tos.

Option B: unlabeled-target structure route.

- Use only unlabeled target audio to audit target feature regions, not labels.
- Design a source training intervention that preserves source supervision but avoids source regions absent from both external targets.
- This may be claimable only if framed carefully as target-unlabeled adaptation rather than source-only generalization.

Current recommendation:

- Prefer Option A first. Candidate D and E both show that scalar KD reweighting is too weak under this shift.
