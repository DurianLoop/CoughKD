## Important adjacent collision found

GLoRIA:

- Mehralian et al., "GLoRIA: Gated Low-Rank Interpretable Adaptation for Dialectal ASR"
- Accepted to ICASSP 2026
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02464

Why it matters:

- It is audio/speech.
- It is metadata-gated adaptation.
- It is close enough that the paper must not claim first metadata-gated audio adaptation.

Why it is not a direct collision:

- It adapts ASR representations through metadata-gated low-rank experts.
- It is not cough or respiratory transfer.
- It is not a deployment-time router between pre-existing transfer strategies.
- It does not classify metadata fields as semantically safe or unsafe for slice gating.
- It does not test symptom/acquisition vs demographic-only routing with inverted semantic controls.
