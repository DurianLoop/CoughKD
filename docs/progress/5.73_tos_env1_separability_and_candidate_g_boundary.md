# 5.73 Tos Env1 Separability and Candidate G Boundary

## Purpose

Continue after 5.72 without launching another expensive training run.

The question was:

> Is Tos env 1 a target slice where unlabeled target structure or artifact-aware adaptation can plausibly recover COVID ranking?

If the answer is no, Candidate G should not be trained yet.

## Related-Work Boundary

The candidate space overlaps with established target-unlabeled adaptation:

- Partial Adversarial Domain Adaptation, ECCV 2018: `https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2018/papers_ECCV/html/Zhangjie_Cao_Partial_Adversarial_Domain_ECCV_2018_paper.php`
- Universal/partial/open-set label-set mismatch overview: `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8620052/`
- TENT test-time entropy minimization, ICLR 2021: `https://openreview.net/forum?id=uXl3bZLkr3c`

These references already cover source-private class suppression, target-unlabeled adaptation, and test-time entropy minimization at the general method level. Therefore Candidate G cannot be claimed as "unlabeled target adaptation for label-set/domain shift" in general.

A defensible novelty claim would need to be narrower, for example:

- cough-audio-specific shift localization,
- ultra-light KD with explicit external-target failure guards,
- or a target-slice diagnostic that prevents negative transfer.

- A target-unlabeled adaptation route has a low chance of producing a claimable 3-5 point gain on Tos without target labels or new data.

## Candidate G Decision

Do not train Candidate G yet.

Rejected low-confidence variants:

- simple partial-domain source-private suppression,
- source-private probability projection,
- native binary remapping,
- artifact-env-only invariance,
- target-env test-time entropy minimization,
- target-unlabeled clustering on current source-trained embeddings.

Reason:

- The necessary target slice either is not separable in artifact features or is only weakly separable in embeddings.
- Existing literature already owns the broad target-unlabeled/PDA/TTA framing.
- A negative Tos result after another adaptation run would be unsurprising and costly.

- Claim: external cough-COVID KD gains are slice-dependent and can be audited before deployment.

- This is the most honest path toward a positive ICASSP 2027 result.

Option C: move to foundation-audio embeddings before inventing a method.

- Audit BEATs/AST/PANNs frozen embeddings on Tos env 1.
- If frozen foundation embeddings exceed env-1 oracle AUROC 0.60 without fine-tuning, then design a lightweight KD method around that teacher.
- If they also fail, stop Tos-positive-claim attempts.

Current recommendation:

- Choose Option C as the next low-token, low-compute gate.
- Do not do more custom KD training until a frozen foundation embedding upper bound clears 0.60 on Tos env 1 and improves all-Tos oracle AUROC beyond the current 0.564254 source-CE upper bound.
