## Matrix contents

The matrix compares the semantic-router claim against these adjacent families:

1. Symptom-assisted cough modeling.
2. Multimodal respiratory metadata modeling.
3. Recent multi-dataset cough adaptation, including CoughSense.
4. Metadata-gated audio adaptation, including GLoRIA.
5. Semantic descriptors for domain adaptation.
6. Negative-transfer and safe-transfer literature.
7. Cross-dataset cough classifiers.
8. KD under distribution shift.
9. Respiratory KD and respiratory foundation representations.

For each family, the matrix records:

- why the work is close;
- why it is not a direct collision;
- which broad novelty claim must be avoided.
