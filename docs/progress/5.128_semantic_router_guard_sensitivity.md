## Main finding

The no-inner-guard semantic rule remains the headline result:

| guard | COUGHVID mean | TosCOVID mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| none | +3.75pt | +3.99pt |
| 0.000 | +3.42pt | +2.33pt |
| 0.005 | +3.06pt | +2.17pt |
| 0.010 | +2.91pt | +2.08pt |

Interpretation:

- guarded variants remain positive on both targets;
- the guard reduces risky deployment frequency but lowers mean gain, especially
  on TosCOVID;
- therefore the inner guard should be framed as a conservative deployment
  option, not as the headline claim.
