## Decision Boundary

These subsets are low-cost probes, not final paper claims. If a small official-train subset gives a positive 1--3 point official-test signal, it justifies running larger calibration tiers. If it fails, stop before spending compute on the full 4,803-row route and continue the innovation/literature loop.
