# 5.97 Source-anchored confidence routing negative result

## Purpose

After the COUGHVID metadata-gate route reached diminishing returns, this note tests a non-metadata, prediction-level candidate:

> keep source-only as the default prediction, and switch an individual target example to a candidate model only when the candidate is more confident or lower-entropy by a fixed margin.

This is meant to be stricter than target-level model selection and independent of COUGHVID symptom metadata.

## Literature boundary

Recent work makes broad non-metadata adaptation claims risky:

- CoughSense uses Whisper-tiny, FiLM symptom conditioning, balanced contrastive learning, and gradient-reversal domain adaptation for respiratory disease classification.
- A 2025 JMIR drift-adaptive COVID cough framework already studies drift detection plus adaptation strategies on COVID cough audio.
- 2026 DNDT/DNDF work reports cross-dataset COVID cough evaluation across Cambridge, Coswara, COUGHVID, Virufy, and merged sets.

Therefore a confidence-routing result would need real cross-target gains to be claimable; a small selector trick is not novel enough by itself.

## Interpretation

This route fails as a claimable innovation.

The confidence router can improve over source-only on Virufyseg, but Virufyseg has only 121 clips from 16 subjects and has already been treated as a small stress target. On the two large targets, the best gains are far below the requested 3-5 point range:

- COUGHVID: best +0.33 points
- TosCOVID: best +0.07 points

This also reinforces an earlier pattern: unlabeled confidence, entropy, prior shape, and prediction geometry are not reliable enough to choose or route cough audio models under target shift.

## Current search implication

Do not promote source-anchored confidence routing as an ICASSP method. It is useful negative evidence, but not a paper contribution.

At this point, the local no-download loops have rejected:

- broad metadata leaf gating;
- symptom-slice macro robustness;
- label-free target-level prediction geometry selection;
- confidence/entropy selective coverage as a full-target method;
- source-anchored confidence routing.

The next meaningful route should be one of:

1. obtain a second target with audio and metadata, preferably Cambridge or UKCOVID;
2. move to a genuinely new training-time mechanism grounded in current literature, not prediction-level post-processing;
3. reframe the paper as a rigorous failure/audit contribution if second-target acquisition remains unavailable.
